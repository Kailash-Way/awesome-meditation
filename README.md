## Apps

### Free
- [Insight Timer](https://insighttimer.com/) - Largest free meditation community.
- [Medito](https://meditofoundation.org/) - 100% free, open-source meditation app.
- [Smiling Mind](https://www.smilingmind.com.au/) - Free mindfulness app by Australian non-profit.
- [Healthy Minds Program](https://hminnovations.org/meditation-app) - Free, science-based app from UW-Madison.

### Paid
- [Calm](https://www.calm.com/) - Popular app for sleep, meditation, and relaxation.

- [Waking Up](https://wakingup.com/) - Sam Harris's meditation and philosophy app.
- [Ten Percent Happier](https://tenpercent.com/) - Meditation for skeptics.
- [Simple Habit](https://www.simplehabit.com/) - 5-minute meditations for busy people.

## Symbols & Meanings

### Tibetan Buddhist Eight Auspicious Symbols
- **Lotus (Padma)** — Purity and enlightenment
- **Dharma Wheel (Dharmachakra)** — Buddha's teachings
- **Endless Knot (Shrivatsa)** — Interdependence
- **Golden Fishes (Suvarnamatsya)** — Courage and contentment
- **Treasure Vase (Kalasha)** — Long life and wealth
- **Parasol (Chatra)** — Protection from harm
- **Conch Shell (Shankha)** — Power of Buddha's voice
- **Vajra (Dorje)** — Indestructible wisdom

### Additional Sacred Symbols
- **Om (ༀ)** — The primordial sound
- **Svastika** — Eternal auspiciousness (Buddhist context)
- **Kalachakra (Tenfold Power)** — Cycles of time
- **Buddha Eyes (Wisdom Eyes)** — Omniscience

### Other Traditions
- **Hamsa Hand** — Protection across cultures
- **Evil Eye** — Ward off negative energy
- **Tree of Life** — Interconnection of all life
- **Mandala** — Cosmic order and wholeness
- **Yantra** — Sacred geometry

> For an open-source SVG icon library of these symbols, see [Tibetan Symbols](https://github.com/Kailash-Way/tibetan-symbols).
